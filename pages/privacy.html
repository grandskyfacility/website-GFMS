Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us — plainly and without legalese wherever possible.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

GrandSky Facility & Management Services LLP ("GrandSky", "we", "us") provides integrated facility services and equipment supply from New Delhi, India. This policy explains what information we collect through grandskyfacility.com and how we use it.

2. What information we collect

  • Enquiry details you provide — name, company/organisation, email, phone number and your message when you submit our contact form or request a quotation.
  • Communication records — emails, calls and site-survey notes in the normal course of business.
  • Basic website data — standard server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) used for security and site improvement. We do not run advertising trackers.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotations, proposals and site surveys.
  • To deliver contracted services, including statutory and compliance documentation.
  • To send service updates you have asked for. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

4. Who processes it with us

We keep data sharing to the minimum needed to run the business: our form provider processes submissions on our behalf, and our own staff, supervisors and accountants handle data for delivery, payroll and statutory purposes. Where third-party processors are used, they are bound to handle the data only on our instructions.

5. Retention & security

Enquiry records are retained only as long as useful for the relationship and for applicable statutory requirements. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards (access control, HTTPS) appropriate to a service business of our size.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Write to customer@grandskyfacility.com or call +91 98100 31191 and we will act on verified requests within a reasonable period.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and revise the "last updated" date. Material changes affecting existing clients will also be communicated directly.
